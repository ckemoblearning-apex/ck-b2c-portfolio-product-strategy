Product Portfolio Strategy
A product portfolio strategy is a high-level plan that helps us manage all of its products in a way that aligns with business goals, market opportunities, and internal capabilities. It sets the direction for how the entire product portfolio should evolve — whether that means investing in new products, sunsetting end-of-life products , or finding the right balance between core and experimental initiatives.

Product Risk Register
Risk assessment across the four dimensions — Value, Usability, Feasibility, and Business Viability — mapped to each journey domain
Risk Framework Reference (Cagan)
Value Risk
Will customers choose to use it? Owner: Product Manager
Usability Risk
Can users figure out how to use it? Owner: Product Designer
Feasibility Risk
Can we build it? Technical capability & constraints. Owner: Lead Engineer
Viability Risk
Does it work for the business? GTM, cost, legal, brand. Owner: Product Manager
Risk Assessment by Opportunity
OpportunityDomainRisk TypeSeverityDescriptionMitigation
Roaming experience opacity Roaming Value Medium Pricing opacity and authentication inconsistency for non-native users creates distrust and discourages repeat use. OCPI-based roaming improvements in evaluation. Transparent pricing pre-session is unexplored.
Plug & Charge (ISO 15118-20) Get to Station & Start Feasibility Medium Certificate-based authentication requires vehicle OEM adoption and charger firmware updates. External dependency not within Circle K's control. Evaluation stage. Monitor OEM adoption curves. ISO 15118-2 (MAC-based) already live as interim.
In-car display integration Charging Session Feasibility Low Requires OCPP session data combined with vehicle API access. Both are externally dependent and market is fragmented across OEMs. Discovery only. Not a near-term priority. Monitor standardisation progress.
1CP resource re-prioritisation Programme-level Viability High Re-prioritisation of resources to 1CP assessed as high likelihood of impact on the B2C GVSL Roadmap. Scope and timeline of affected initiatives not yet fully defined. Escalate to eMM. Clarify which B2C GVSL items are at risk and reprioritise accordingly.
GMAP convergence — customer migration & GTM GMAP Convergence & Commercial Viability High Converging away from the Circle K Charge App risks increased customer friction and potential customer loss for users not successfully migrated to GMAP. Commercial GTM model not yet confirmed. Define migration plan and rollback strategy. Validate GTM approach before full rollout. Phased market launch (Latvia/Lithuania Tap & Pay first) agreed as interim mitigation.
